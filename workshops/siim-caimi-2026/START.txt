==============================================================
  Welcome to the workshop!
==============================================================

  Your workshop files are in ~/workshop-imaging. In this
  terminal, run:

             cd ~/workshop-imaging
             kiro-cli

  No Kiro sign-in is needed in the AWS Workshop Studio
  environment.

  Notes
  - Your AWS account is temporary (72 hours). Files are deleted
    afterward, so download anything you want to keep.
  - Stuck? Ask a helper in the room.
